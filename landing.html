Engineering for Reality

Four Reasons WardVault Solves the PHC Implementation Gap

Most hospital software expects an urban teaching hospital. WardVault is built from the ground up for the resource-constrained facility.

📡

Local-First, Not Merely Mobile

A facility laptop acts as the local hub. CHEW tablets connect over a local Wi-Fi router with zero internet required. Changes queue in encrypted IndexedDB and sync automatically when network returns.

📄

Managed Paper Migration

PHCs cannot throw away paper folders. WardVault includes a structured intake workflow with a critical safeguard: clerks flag illegible notes for clinical review rather than guessing handwriting.

🔄

Care Coordination

PHCs must transfer complex cases. WardVault generates secondary transfer slips to General Hospitals or UCH and tracks patient attendance, closing the primary-secondary feedback loop.

🔋

Hardware Power Alarms

With 38.7% of PHCs experiencing blackouts, WardVault monitors battery levels through the Web Audio & Battery API, alerting nurses with audio tones to switch to solar backup before devices die.

Regulatory Trust

Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 Built In

Health records involve children, mothers, and sensitive diagnoses. WardVault does not rely on standard database logs that can be secretly modified by anyone with root access.

🔒 Cryptographic Hash Chaining: Each consultation and note is chained to the previous record with SHA-256. Altering any past record breaks the chain instantly.
📝 Append-Only Amendments: Staff cannot retroactively overwrite notes; clinical corrections link via supersedes_note_id with audited clinical reasons.
🛡️ SIEM Command Center: Independent dashboard for the Chief Privacy Officer to scan and verify ledger integrity with one click.
