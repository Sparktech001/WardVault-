3-Stage Paper-to-Digital Transition Strategy

How Primary Healthcare Centres transition smoothly from physical paper folders to digital medical records with zero data loss and continuous patient safety.

1

Layer 1: Digital-First

All New Patients Go 100% Digital

Any newborn or first-time patient registering at the PHC receives a digital folder directly. No physical paper folder is created, completely halting paper folder growth.

2

Layer 2: Parallel Dual-Track

Returning Paper Cards Run in Parallel

Patients who present an existing physical paper card have their demographic and clinical data transcribed digitally on arrival while keeping the paper card intact.

3

Layer 3: Archival Catch-Up

Off-Peak Reconciliation & Review

Records staff transcribe historic archive folders during quiet hours. Faded ink, illegible dosages, or ambiguous entries are automatically flagged for nurse sign-off.
